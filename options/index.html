Payoff at expiry

P&L IF EXPIRY AT—
How it's calculated: at expiry a call is worth max(price − strike, 0) and a put max(strike − price, 0). Each leg's P&L = (value at expiry − premium) × lots × lot size, flipped for sold legs. Futures P&L = (price − entry) × quantity. It shows the payoff at expiry only, before charges and taxes. Before expiry, option prices also depend on time left and volatility.
