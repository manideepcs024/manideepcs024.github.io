🧨 How the trap worked

1Quiet buying: operators collect a cheap, illiquid penny stock with tiny volumes.
2Hype: thousands are added to “free tips” groups. Fake profit screenshots, “SEBI registered ✅”, “big news”, urgency and secrecy.
3Pump: buy orders from the group push it into daily upper circuits. It looks like proof.
4Dump: operators sell all their shares to the group's buyers. The admin goes silent.
5Trap: lower circuits with only sellers. You can't exit, and the group disappears.

🛡️ Stay safe: do this every time

  • Check registration on SEBI's website (Intermediaries → Research Analysts / Investment Advisers). Real numbers start with INH (RA) or INA (IA).
  • Nobody can promise fixed or guaranteed returns. “Operator call”, “insider news” and “100% accuracy” are lies or illegal.
  • Read the company's results and announcements on NSE / BSE. No filing means no “big news”.
  • Check whether the stock is on the exchange's ASM / GSM surveillance lists.
  • Huge volume and upper circuits with no news? That's a pump. Stay away.
  • Never pay for tips to a personal UPI ID, and never share OTPs or screen-sharing access.
