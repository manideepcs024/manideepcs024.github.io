📒 Fundamentals cheat sheet

  • Market cap = share price × total shares. It shows the company's size; price alone doesn't.
  • EPS = net profit ÷ shares. P/E = price ÷ EPS: what you pay for ₹1 of yearly profit. Compare within the same industry, and against growth.
  • Book value per share = (assets − liabilities) ÷ shares. P/B = price ÷ BVPS. Most useful for banks and asset-heavy firms.
  • Shareholding: steady or rising promoter holding is good. Steady promoter selling or rising pledges is a warning.
  • Pledged shares can be sold by lenders if the price falls, which can push it down further.
  • A cheap P/E isn't a bargain if profits are shrinking. That's a value trap.
