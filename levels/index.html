How it's calculated: Pivot (P) = (H + L + C) ÷ 3. Classic: R1 = 2P − L, S1 = 2P − H, R2 = P + (H − L), S2 = P − (H − L), R3 = H + 2(P − L), S3 = L − 2(H − P). Fibonacci: R/S = P ± 0.382, 0.618 and 1.0 × (H − L). CPR: BC = (H + L) ÷ 2, TC = 2P − BC. A narrow CPR (under 0.2% of price) often comes before a trending day; a wide CPR (over 0.5%) often comes before a sideways day. These are general observations, not predictions.
