Results & concalls, decoded.

Four times a year every listed company reports results, and stocks can jump or crash 10% in a day. The secret: the price reacts to the surprise, not the number. Learn to read a result in 5 minutes, what to listen for on the earnings call, and how to protect yourself around results day.

🗓️ How results season works

QUARTER ENDS30 Jun · 30 Sep · 31 Dec · 31 MarCompanies close their books (Q1 to Q4 of the financial year).
BOARD MEETING NOTICEDate announcedThe company tells the exchange when its board will approve results. Insiders' trading window closes.
RESULTS DAYWithin 45 daysQuarterly results are filed on NSE/BSE, usually within 45 days (60 days for the Q4/annual results).
CONCALLSame day or nextManagement explains the numbers and answers analysts. Audio and transcript are filed later.
REACTIONNext sessionThe stock reprices on the surprise and on guidance. Moves can continue for days ('post-results drift').

📄 Reading a result in 5 minutes

Open the results filing (or a site like Screener) and check these, always year-on-year (YoY) against the same quarter last year, because many businesses are seasonal.

Revenue (sales)Is the business growing? Compare YoY. Split into volume (more units) vs price (higher prices) if the company tells you.Look for: steady double-digit growth
EBITDA & marginOperating profit before interest, tax and depreciation. Margin = EBITDA ÷ revenue shows pricing power and cost control.Look for: stable or rising margin
Other incomeInterest, dividends, one-time gains. Not from the core business.Red flag: a big part of profit comes from here
Exceptional itemsOne-offs: asset sales, write-offs, legal settlements. Remove them to see the real trend.Red flag: profit growth driven by one-offs
Net profit (PAT) & EPSThe bottom line. EPS = profit per share, used for P/E.Look for: PAT growing with revenue, not faster only because of tax or one-offs
Segments & order bookWhich divisions grew? For capital goods, defence or infra: new orders and the order book.Look for: the main segment driving growth
Cash & debt (half-yearly)Balance sheet and cash flow come every 6 months. Profit should turn into cash.Red flag: rising receivables or debt
QoQ checkCompare with the last quarter for momentum, but remember seasonality (e.g. cement Q2 is weak).Look for: improving trend

🎯 Why "good results" can sink a stock

Analysts publish estimates before results, and the price already reflects them. The stock moves on the gap between actual and expected, and on what management says about the future.

🛡️ For traders: around results day

  • Check the results calendar before entering any swing trade.
  • Avoid fresh positions the day before results unless you accept a gap of 10%+.
  • Holding into results? Reduce size: a stop-loss can't protect you from a gap.
  • Option sellers: don't sell naked options into results; option buyers: premiums are inflated and collapse after the event (IV crush).
  • Let the first 30–60 minutes after results settle before reacting.

📈 For investors: using results well

  • Judge the trend over 4–8 quarters, not one quarter.
  • Track whether management delivers on its guidance; credibility compounds.
  • A fall on a temporary issue in a great business can be an opportunity; a fall on structural problems is a warning.
  • Watch "buy the rumour, sell the news": stocks often rise into results and fall after, even on good numbers.
  • Read the full transcript for the businesses you own.
