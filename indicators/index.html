Indicators turn price and volume into a clearer picture of trend and momentum. Pick one below: it's calculated live on a practice chart (computer-generated, not a real stock). Change the settings and press New chart to see how it behaves in different markets.

🧩 A simple way to combine them

  1. Trend first (EMAs): trade long only when price is above the 200 EMA and the 50 is above the 200.
  2. Strength (ADX): prefer trades when ADX is above ~20–25, so there's a real trend to ride.
  3. Timing (RSI / MACD): buy pullbacks where RSI holds 40–50 and turns up, or MACD crosses up above zero.
  4. Intraday (VWAP): longs above VWAP, shorts below it.
  5. Exit (Supertrend / 21 EMA): trail your stop with the Supertrend line or a close below the 21 EMA.

Indicators are derived from price, so they lag. Use 2–3, not 10, and always respect your stop-loss. Then check candles and patterns: candlesticks · chart patterns.
