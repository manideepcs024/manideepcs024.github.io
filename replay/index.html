🧭 How to practise well

📏 Decide before you clickKnow your setup (pattern, EMA trend, breakout), your stop and your target before entering. The ticket fills a suggestion; change it to your plan.
⚖️ Risk 1% per tradeThe quantity is calculated so a stop-loss hit costs about 1% of your capital. Ten losers in a row still leave you 90% of your money.
✂️ Don't remove the stopMoving a stop further away is how small losses become big ones. Trail it in your favour only.
🧘 Sitting out is allowedNo clear setup? Press Next. Fewer, better trades usually grade higher than many random ones.
🔁 RepeatPlay 10 sessions and compare. Look at your log: which setups worked, and where did you break rules?
🧪 Then go real, smallPaper trading has no fear or greed. When you move to real money, start with tiny size.
