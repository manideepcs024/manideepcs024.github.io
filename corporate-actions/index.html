📆 The dates that matter

Since India moved to T+1 settlement, the ex-date and the record date are the same day. To get a dividend, bonus or split, you must buy on or before the trading day before the record date. Buying on the record date itself is too late.

🧠 Common mistakes

"Price halved, I lost 50%!"After a 1:1 bonus or 1:2 split, you have double the shares. Check the value, not the price. Some apps show a temporary loss until the new shares are credited.
Buying just for the dividendThe price drops by about the dividend on the ex-date, and the dividend is taxed at your slab. It's not free money.
Bonus = free wealth?No. A bonus only moves money from reserves to share capital. It can improve liquidity and sentiment, but the business is the same.
Ignoring a rights issueIf you neither apply nor sell your rights entitlements (REs), they lapse worthless and your holding gets diluted.
Wrong tax cost after a demergerSplit your original cost between the two companies in the ratio the company announces, or your capital gains will be wrong.
Stop-losses after an adjustmentBrokers may cancel or not adjust open orders (GTT, SL) on the ex-date. Re-check them.
