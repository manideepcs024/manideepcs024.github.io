How set-off works: short-term losses can reduce both short- and long-term gains; long-term losses can reduce only long-term gains. This tool uses short-term losses against short-term gains first, because those are taxed at the higher rate. The LTCG exemption applies to what's left. Losses that remain can be carried forward for 8 years if you file your return on time. Not covered: surcharge, the basic exemption limit and rebates, grandfathering for shares bought before February 2018, intraday or F&O income (taxed as business income), and debt funds.
