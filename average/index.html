How it's calculated: Average price = total money invested ÷ total shares, where total money invested = Σ (price × quantity). Target average: shares to buy = held qty × (current avg − target) ÷ (target − buy price), rounded up to a whole share. Remember: averaging down lowers your break-even, but it also puts more money into a stock that is falling. Only average into a position you'd still buy fresh today, and keep a stop-loss on the whole position.
