How the grade works: Discipline (30%): stop-loss followed, plan followed, no revenge or overtrading, checklist GO before trades. Risk control (25%): losses kept near −1R, daily max loss respected. Edge (20%): expectancy in R per trade. Review habit (15%): trading days you reviewed. Consistency (10%): green days out of days traded. Parts with no data are left out and the rest re-weighted. R = profit or loss ÷ the risk you planned (entry to stop-loss). Charges aren't included unless you entered them in the day review.
