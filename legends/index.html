🧵 What every legend had in common

✂️ Small, fast lossesO'Neil: 7–8% max. Minervini: often tighter. Livermore went broke when he broke this rule.
🏃 Let winners runBig money comes from a few big winners held long enough: "my sitting," as Livermore put it.
📐 Position sizingThe Turtles risked a fixed small % per trade. Size decides whether a bad streak hurts or kills.
📈 Trade with the trendWeinstein's Stage 2, PTJ's 200-day average, Minervini's template: all keep you on the right side.
📓 A written processEvery one of them had rules, and a journal. Discipline beats prediction.
🧠 Psychology firstDouglas: think in probabilities. Any single trade can lose; the edge plays out over many.
