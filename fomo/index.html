🧠 Why these words work on us

⏰ Urgency"Last chance", "only today", "jaldi". A deadline switches off careful thinking. Real opportunities rarely disappear in 10 minutes.
🤑 Greed & guarantees"100% sure", "paisa double", "multibagger". Markets have no guarantees; anyone promising one is either wrong or lying.
🤫 Secrecy"Operator buying", "inside news". Trading on real inside information is illegal; the 'secret' is usually the scam.
👥 Fake social proof"Everyone is buying", profit screenshots. Screenshots are easy to fake and only the winners are shown.
💳 Pay to join"VIP group", "limited seats", "DM now". Selling stock tips needs SEBI registration. Most paid tip groups don't have it.
🎯 Fixed targets"Target ₹500 in 2 days". Exact quick targets with no stop-loss or risk are a red flag, not analysis.

✅ Follow professional information

Before acting on any tip, check where it comes from. These are the sources professionals use.

🏛️ Exchange filingsResults, orders, bonuses and announcements are filed on NSE and BSE (Corporate Announcements). If it's not filed, it's a rumour.
🏢 Company sourcesAnnual reports, investor presentations and concall transcripts on the company's investor-relations page.
🪪 Check SEBI registrationAnyone giving buy/sell advice for money must be a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. Search their name on SEBI's registered intermediaries list.
📊 Your own processRun it through your pre-trade checklist: chart, results date, risk-reward, stop-loss.
📣 Report fraudComplain about unregistered advice on SEBI SCORES. For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 or use cybercrime.gov.in.
⏳ The 24-hour ruleIf a tip makes you excited, wait a day. If it's still a good idea after research, it'll still be there.
