They fell. They rose.

A superstar buried in debt. A future billionaire who borrowed $4,000 from his dad. A founder fired from his own company. Real people who hit rock bottom and came back, and a few who didn't. Read one on a bad trading day.

🔁 What every comeback had in common

Different fields, same pattern. These are the habits that turn a fall into a comeback, in life and in trading.

1. They survived firstBachchan paid off his debts. Dalio cut his firm down to himself. Before you can win again, you must stay in the game: small size, stop-losses, no all-in bets.
2. They owned the mistakeNone of them blamed the market or bad luck forever. They asked "what did I do wrong?" and changed it. That's your journal and lesson book.
3. They changed the method, not the dreamMurthy's first company failed; he started another. Jobs built NeXT. Keep the goal, fix the process.
4. They gave it timeMost comebacks took years, not days. One trade won't fix one bad month. Steady, disciplined trades will.
5. They didn't cheat to get backThe cautionary tales all share one thing: shortcuts, hidden losses or reckless leverage. There's no comeback from that.
