Read the candles.

Each candle tells the story of a battle between buyers and sellers in one period (a minute, a day, a week). Learn the four prices inside every candle, then the patterns traders watch at support and resistance.

Green / hollow = close above open (buyers won). Red / filled = close below open (sellers won). The thick part is the body (open to close); the thin lines are wicks or shadows (how far price travelled but got pushed back).

🧭 How to use candles properly

📍 Location beats shapeA hammer at strong support after a fall means a lot. The same shape in the middle of nowhere means little.
✅ Wait for confirmationThe next candle should agree: a bullish close above the pattern's high, ideally on higher volume.
📈 Respect the trendPatterns in the direction of the bigger trend (e.g. bullish engulfing in an uptrend pullback) work better than fighting it.
⏱️ Bigger timeframe = strongerA daily or weekly pattern carries more weight than a 5-minute one.
🛑 Use the candle for the stopFor a bullish pattern, the stop-loss usually goes just below its low; for a bearish one, just above its high.
🧩 CombineCandles + support/resistance + volume + trend (EMAs) beats any candle alone. See indicators and chart patterns.
