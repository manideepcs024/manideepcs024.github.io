📥 How to download the files from Zerodha Console
  1. Open console.zerodha.com and log in.
  2. Reports → Tradebook: choose segment (Equity / F&O), pick the date range, and download as XLSX or CSV.
  3. Reports → P&L: choose the same segment and range, and download as XLSX (it includes charges and other debits).
  4. Upload both here. Add more tradebooks (other years or F&O) anytime; duplicate trades are skipped automatically.
