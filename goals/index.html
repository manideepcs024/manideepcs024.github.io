Equity return % long term
How it's worked out: Future cost = today's cost × (1 + inflation)years. Your existing savings grow at the expected return. SIP needed assumes you invest at the start of every month (monthly compounding). Step-up raises the SIP every year by the % you choose, so it starts lower and grows with your income. Retirement: your monthly expenses are inflated to retirement age, then the corpus must fund them (still rising with inflation) until the life expectancy you set, earning the post-retirement return. Expected returns are assumptions, not promises: equity is volatile year to year.
