All accounts, one P&L.

Set up each broking account once (holder, broker, client ID). Every month, enter the net profit or loss per segment from the broker's P&L report. You get the total for each account, each person, each segment and all accounts together, ready to share. Use a minus sign for a loss (e.g. -12,500). Everything stays on this device.

Segments tap to show or hide

Tip: take the net realised P&L per segment from each broker's P&L / tax report (after charges if the report shows it). Turn on the Charges segment only if your numbers are before charges.
