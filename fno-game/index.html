📒 F&O cheat sheet

  • Expect a rise: buy CE. Expect a fall: buy PE. A buyer's maximum loss is the premium paid.
  • Futures move ₹1 × lot size per point, both ways. They need a big margin and have no loss limit.
  • Premium = intrinsic value + time value. Time value melts every day (theta), fastest near expiry.
  • At expiry only intrinsic value is left, so out-of-the-money options become ₹0.
  • Book profits or trail a stop-loss. Options can double and then halve within days.
  • Trade liquid strikes: check volume, open interest and the bid-ask spread. Use limit orders.
  • Always use a stop-loss, and size so a gap past it won't wipe you out.

⚠️ Reality check

SEBI's 2024 study of individual F&O traders found that roughly 9 in 10 lost money. Learn on paper first, risk only what you can afford to lose, and never trade F&O with borrowed money.
