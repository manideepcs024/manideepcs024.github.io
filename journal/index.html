How it's calculated: Risk per share = |entry − stop-loss|. R:R = |target − entry| ÷ risk. Result in R = profit per share ÷ risk per share, so +2R means you made twice what you risked and −1R means you lost exactly what you planned. Break-even win rate for an R:R of 1:x is 1 ÷ (1 + x). Charges and taxes aren't included.
