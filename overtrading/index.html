How to stop overtrading

🎯Decide before 9:15: daily profit target, max loss and max number of trades. Write them down.
🔒Target hit → log off. Close the trading app, close the charts, and leave the desk.
🌳Go out. A walk, chai, the gym, family time. Distance from the screen protects the profit.
✋Two losses in a row → stop for the day. The third trade is usually the angry one.
⚖️Never increase size to "win it back", never move the stop-loss, never average a loser.
📉Protect the peak: if you give back 30% of the day's best P&L, stop.
📝Review the day in the evening. Good days and bad days both teach.
