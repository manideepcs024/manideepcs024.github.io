The plan

How it's worked out: payoff is at expiry, per share × lots × lot size. Probability of profit uses a simple log-normal model from your IV and days to expiry; real moves have fatter tails, so treat it as a rough guide. Gap test shows the P&L if the stock opens 15% away tomorrow. Estimated premiums use Black-Scholes; always trade on live prices. Charges and taxes aren't included.
