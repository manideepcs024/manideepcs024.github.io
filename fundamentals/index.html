Reading a company, line by line.

Every company tells its story in three statements. Learn to read them with a simple example company, understand the ratios that matter, spot the red flags, then check any stock with the health checker.

🧾 The three statements in one minute

PROFIT & LOSSDid it make money?Sales minus all costs, over a year or quarter. Shows growth and margins.
BALANCE SHEETWhat does it own and owe?A snapshot on one date: assets, borrowings and the shareholders' money.
CASH FLOWDid real cash come in?Profit can be on paper. Cash flow shows if customers actually paid and where the cash went.

📏 The ratios that matter (calculated for Sunrise Foods)

Ratios turn the statements into quick comparisons. Always compare with the company's own history and with peers in the same industry. A good margin for a retailer is a poor one for software.

🩺 Stock health checker

Take the numbers from a site like Screener.in (top ratios and the "compounded growth" tables) or the annual report. Leave a box empty to skip that check. For banks and NBFCs, use different measures (GNPA, NIM, CASA, capital adequacy); debt ratios don't apply.

🚩 Red flags to look for

💸 Profit without cashNet profit rises every year but operating cash flow stays low. Check if receivables (money customers owe) are growing faster than sales.
🏦 Rising debt + falling coverageBorrowings growing faster than profit, and interest coverage dropping below 2–3×.
🔒 Promoter pledgePromoters borrowing against their shares. If the price falls, lenders can sell, pushing it down further.
🧾 Auditor resignsEspecially mid-term or with comments on governance. One of the strongest warnings.
🤝 Related-party dealsLarge purchases, loans or sales with promoter-owned firms. Read the notes in the annual report.
🎁 Other income carrying profitIf a big part of profit comes from 'other income' (interest, one-time gains), core business may be weak.
🏗️ Endless CWIPCapital work-in-progress that never turns into working assets: money spent with no output.
📉 Frequent dilutionRepeated QIPs, preferential issues or warrants to promoters at low prices shrink your share of the company.
📜 Contingent liabilitiesBig tax demands or legal claims in the notes that could hit future profits.

📚 Where to find the data

📕 Annual reportThe full story: statements, notes, management discussion, related-party details. On the company website and NSE/BSE.
📊 Quarterly resultsFiled on NSE/BSE within weeks of quarter-end. Read with the investor presentation.
🧮 Screener / TickertapeFree sites that summarise 10 years of numbers and ratios. Great for a first look; confirm in the filings.
🎙️ Concall transcriptsWhat management says about demand, margins, capacity and guidance. See our Results guide.
🏷️ Credit ratingsCRISIL, ICRA, CARE reports explain a company's debt and risks in plain language.
📋 Shareholding patternPromoter, FII, DII and public holding every quarter, including pledged shares. On NSE/BSE.
