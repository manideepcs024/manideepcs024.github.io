What is front-running?

Front-running means buying (or selling) ahead of information you know will move the price, then trading against the people who act on it. With TV or social-media tips, someone who knows a stock will be recommended buys it before the tip goes public, then sells to the viewers who rush in after it. It is market manipulation and illegal in India; SEBI has investigated and penalised people for exactly this pattern.

🚩 Red flags of a tip trap

📈Already up a lot in the days before the tip, often 15% or more.
📊Volume spike before the news was public.
🐜Small or illiquid stock: easy to push up and hard to exit.
⏰Urgency + big target: "buy NOW", "target ₹160 soon".
🧱Big sell orders waiting above the price while it gaps up.
🪪No SEBI registration: check an adviser's or analyst's registration on SEBI's website.

What to do instead

⏳Never buy at market into a TV or WhatsApp spike. Wait a few days; real opportunities don't vanish in an hour.
🔍Run your own checks: fundamentals, the chart before the tip, volume and events.
🎯Limit orders + stop-loss, always, and a size you can afford to be wrong on.
📣Suspect manipulation? Don't forward it. You can report it to SEBI.
