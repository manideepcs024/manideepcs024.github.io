LEVERAGE LAB

Same stock. Three ways to trade it. ₹1,00,000 to trade + ₹50,000 in savings. Which style suits you, and what does leverage really cost?

⚡Intraday: up to 5× leverage, but you must exit the same day, or the broker squares you off.
📦Positional (delivery): your own money, hold as long as you want, no interest.
🏦MTF: the broker lends you money to hold for days or weeks. You pay daily interest and can get a margin call.

📒 Leverage cheat sheet

  • Intraday leverage multiplies both profit and loss. Use a stop-loss and exit before auto square-off.
  • Positional needs patience and full capital, but no one can force you out.
  • MTF interest = funded amount × yearly rate × days ÷ 365. It's charged even if the price doesn't move.
  • A margin call comes when your own money in the trade falls below the required margin. Add funds or reduce the position, or the broker can sell.
  • With 4× exposure, a 10% fall wipes out about 40% of your money in the trade.
  • Never use leverage for long-term investing, and keep spare cash for margin calls.
